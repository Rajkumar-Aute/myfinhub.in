Privacy Policy for My Fin Hub

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

My Fin Hub ("we", "app", or "service") is committed to protecting your privacy. This application is designed with a Zero-Knowledge and Zero-Server architecture. We do not operate external database servers, and we never collect, track, monetize, or sell your personal or financial information.

2. Data Storage and Google Drive

All your transactions, bank accounts, and loans are stored exclusively in two places under your complete control:

  • Local Storage: Stored securely in your own browser's local memory on your device.
  • Your Personal Google Drive: Synced directly to a dedicated private folder (My Fin Hub) inside your personal Google Drive account.

3. Google User Data & Scopes

My Fin Hub uses the Google Drive API with the restricted per-file scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file):

  • The app can only access and create files that My Fin Hub itself creates (myfinhub.json).
  • The app cannot read, see, or modify any other files, photos, or documents in your Google Drive.
  • Authentication tokens remain solely within your local browser session and are never transmitted to any third party.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties, advertisers, analytics trackers, or data brokers. All communication happens directly between your browser and Google APIs over encrypted HTTPS connections.
